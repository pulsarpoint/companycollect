# NOVELIC — public company analysis

Research cut-off: **7 September 2026**. Subject: the business operating at **novelic.com**, principally NOVELIC d.o.o. Beograd–Zvezdara and its subsidiaries. Currency amounts are identified explicitly. This report separates verified disclosures, company claims, secondary records, and analytical judgments.

**Overall assessment**

NOVELIC is an established Serbian radar and embedded engineering company with a credible technical portfolio and a strategically useful majority shareholder, Sona Comstar. Its commercial proposition is to combine radar hardware, signal processing and embedded software, then supply either engineering work or a product that customers can integrate into vehicles, buildings and machines. Named semiconductor partners and disclosed customer projects support its technical credibility.

The central business issue is the transition from engineering projects to repeatable product sales. Public evidence supports automotive nominations, investment in production capability and new applications in robotics. It does not yet establish a profitable, large-volume automotive radar franchise. The latest accounts show deteriorating profitability and cash consumption. The investment case therefore depends on converting technical capability and design wins into shipments with sustainable margins.

My assessment is **positive on technical credibility, cautiously positive on strategic positioning, and cautious on present financial performance**. This is a qualitative assessment, not a valuation or credit rating. The supporting evidence and its limits follow.

**4. Business model and product portfolio**

NOVELIC markets a combination of custom electronics and software engineering, reusable radar platforms, signal-processing software, and semiconductor design. Its services extend across RF and antenna design, FPGA/ASIC work, embedded software, algorithms, sensor fusion, mechanical integration and validation. [Company overview](https://www.novelic.com/about-us/), [semiconductor design services](https://www.novelic.com/semiconductor-design-services/).

The plausible revenue mechanisms are paid engineering and non-recurring development, hardware supply, customization, and software or IP licensing. Public disclosures do not quantify the mix, recurring license income, average selling prices, gross margins by product, or engineering utilization. There is no basis to characterize this as a SaaS business or infer annual recurring revenue.

**How to interpret the finances.** Both reporting bases show a material deterioration in earnings. The group was consuming operating cash while continuing development investment. Cash in the bank alone gives an incomplete picture because short-term financial assets fell sharply. A substantial accounting equity balance provides some balance-sheet protection, but it is not equivalent to readily available cash.

Capitalized development makes the success of future products especially consequential: an asset’s recorded cost must ultimately be supported by economic benefits. This does not prove overstatement or require an impairment today. It means that useful lives, amortization, product-level forecasts and recoverability deserve examination before relying on book equity.

The schedules explicitly contain R&D reclassifications and intercompany eliminations. Some local income therefore does not remain commercial revenue on the group basis. It would be misleading to splice the two tables into a single growth chart, to interpret every local income line as an external customer sale, or to add subsidiary revenues without eliminations.

The loss cannot be confidently attributed to one cause from public summaries. Plausible contributors include the cost of product development, program timing and engineering demand; their individual contributions are not disclosed. Current cash runway, committed shareholder support, uncollected receivables and the profitability of the 2026 production ramp remain unknown.

**9. Quality, safety, privacy and regulatory relevance**

The company states that it obtained ISO 9001:2015 certification in 2018 and also cites ISO 14001:2015. Its quality page describes work toward IATF 16949 prerequisites. I did not establish current IATF 16949, ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification from certificate documents with validity dates and scopes. [Quality-management page](https://www.novelic.com/quality-management/).

Automotive component qualification, an organizational quality certificate and functional-safety certification answer different questions. ACAM’s published QM designation does not mean ASIL certification. Similarly, industrial wording such as “designed to meet” SIL2 or SIL3 is a development objective until an applicable assessment or certificate is supplied. [ACAM specifications](https://www.novelic.com/acam-automotive-in-cabin-monitoring-radar/), [industrial portfolio](https://www.novelic.com/industrial-radar-sensor-solutions/).

Euro NCAP provides a real commercial incentive for occupant monitoring. Its 2026 Safe Driving/Occupant Monitoring protocol allocates five points to child-presence detection and specifies direct sensing and default activation among its requirements. This supports demand for systems that can satisfy those tests. It does not require manufacturers to buy NOVELIC radar, mandate radar as the only sensing method, or make one component sufficient for a five-star vehicle rating. Euro NCAP assessment should also be distinguished from statutory vehicle requirements. [Euro NCAP protocol v1.1, October 2025](https://www.euroncap.com/media/91703/euro-ncap-protocol-safe-driving-occupant-monitoring-v11.pdf).

Radar can reduce the need to capture identifiable visual imagery. However, occupancy or physiological information can still require privacy controls depending on the complete deployment. NOVELIC publishes a privacy policy referring to Serbian law and GDPR principles; the policy is evidence of its stated approach, not proof of product security or operational compliance. [Privacy policy](https://www.novelic.com/privacy-policy/). No internal security assessment, penetration test or vulnerability scan was performed for this research.
