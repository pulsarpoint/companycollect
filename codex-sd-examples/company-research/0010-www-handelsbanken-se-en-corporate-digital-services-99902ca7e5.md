#  Digital corporate services
With our digital banking services, you can easily manage your company's finances whenever and wherever it suits you. Pay invoices, transfer funds, and keep track of your savings and loans.
[Log in to Online Banking](https://secure.handelsbanken.se/logon/se/corp/en/)
![A business man working in a hotel lobby.](/tron/public/info/contents/v1/image/35-275524?variantId=900)
##  Manage your company's banking digitally
  * ###  Online Banking – for everyday banking
Manage your company’s banking online. Control access with mandates, handle both private and business finances as a sole trader, and manage international banking through GlobalOn-Line.
[Online Banking](/en/corporate/digital-services/online-banking)
  * ###  Online Self-Employed – for sole traders
If you are a sole trader you may not need all the services included in Online Banking. Online Self-Employed lets you manage both your personal and business banking in one place. Read more about what’s included in the Self-Employed Package.
[Our corporate packages](/en/corporate/our-corporate-packages)


##  Mobile banking services – banking on the go
  * ###  The corporate mobile app
With our corporate app, the Bank is always close at hand. View balances and transactions, transfer money, make and authorise payments, scan invoices using OCR, and authorise files directly from your mobile phone. Included in the Corporate Package and available in Swedish.
  * ###  App for sole traders
With our app for sole traders, you can manage both personal and business finances in one place. View balances and transactions, transfer money, make payments and scan invoices using OCR. Included in the Self-Employed Package and available in English.
  * ###  App for Business Card users
Manage your Business Card directly in the app. View transactions and available credit, control card settings for online and international purchases, activate new cards, and access benefits and offers. Available in Swedish and can be used by all Business Card holders.


##  Administer authorisations online
Manage who can access your company’s banking services and what they are authorised to do. Register, update and remove authorisations online whenever needed. To administer authorisations online, you need the appropriate permission. Contact your local branch to get started.
Benefits:
  * Allow multiple users to manage banking services
  * The person with administration rights can decide who can do what
  * Authorisations take effect immediately

##  We prioritise your security For your own and your company's sake
  * ###  Ways to log in
With Mobile BankID, your ID is always with you on your phone. You can carry out your most common banking tasks using Mobile BankID. Other log-in methods to our digital services are log-in card with a card reader or personal code.
