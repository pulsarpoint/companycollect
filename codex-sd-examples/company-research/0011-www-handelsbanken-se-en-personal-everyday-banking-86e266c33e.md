##  Accounts For transactions or savings
  * ###  Transaction accounts
The Allkonto account is for your everyday banking transactions. It is where your salary is deposited and from where you make your payments. You can connect a debit card to your account. It is possible to have a joint account with more than one card. You can also set up standing orders from your account or attach a credit. The Allkonto account is part of our basic offering.
[Read more about our basic offering](/en/personal/our-basic-offering)
  * ###  Savings accounts
Depositing money into your savings account is secure, as you do not risk losing any money. And money for unforeseen expenses is good to have if the dishwasher, a tooth or the car breaks down. Why not have different accounts for different savings? There are many different kinds of savings accounts and investment alternatives.


## Covered by the deposit guarantee scheme
The accounts are covered by the deposit guarantee scheme in accordance with a decision by the National Debt Office. Each customer is entitled to compensation for the combined amount on the accounts that they hold, up to a maximum of SEK 1,150,000.
The Swedish National Debt Office makes the compensation available for payment within 7 business days from the date the right to compensation has arisen. In addition to this amount, the account holder may, under certain conditions and according to law, receive compensation for deposits related to specific life events – for example, the sale of a private residence, inheritance, or insurance payout – up to a maximum of SEK 5,000,000.
[Deposit guarantee](/en/about-us/legal-documents/deposit-guarantee)

##  Mobile payments Your digital wallet
  * ###  Apple Pay
Pay with iPhone or Apple Watch. Face ID and Touch ID make payment easy and secure. You can have Apple Pay from the age of 13.
  * ###  Samsung Pay
Collect your cards in the app and pay online or pay in store. You can have Samsung Pay from the age of 16.
