## Banking services your company needs
Our Corporate Package gives your company access to essential banking services in one complete solution. For sole traders, we also offer a Self-Employed Package tailored to the specific needs of running a one-person business.
### The Corporate Package – one size fits all
Handelsbanken’s Corporate Package is designed for all types of businesses. It suits self-employed customers and companies with more advanced needs. The package is easy to start and simple to manage.
You can handle most banking online or by phone, whenever it suits you. You also get up-to-date information and a personal contact at the bank, available by phone, email or in person.
The monthly fee for the Corporate Package is SEK 160 (annual fee SEK 1 920), which includes:
  * Online Corporate Banking (including file services)
  * Corporate account linked to the Bankgiro service
  * Handelsbanken Business Card (Debit or Credit)
  * Personal service by phone, 24/7
  * Mobile banking (in Swedish)
  * Corporate newsletter


Transaction fees are invoiced separately. For more details, please see the price list.
[Conditions and price lists](/en/about-us/legal-documents/terms-and-conditions-corporate)
### The Self-Employed Package – for sole traders
Running your own business should be simple. Our Self-Employed Package gives you the services you need as a sole trader. It ensures you have everything you need for your daily business. You can manage both private and business banking online or by phone, whenever it suits you. You can reach us at the bank 24 hours a day. Our services also make it easier to send and receive payments.
The monthly fee for the Self-Employed Package is SEK 75 (annual fee SEK 900), which includes:
  * Online Banking Self-Employed
  * Corporate account linked to the Bankgiro service
  * Handelsbanken Business Card (Debit or Credit)
  * Personal service by phone, 24/7
  * Mobile banking (in Swedish only)
  * Corporate newsletter


Transaction fees are invoiced separately. For more details, please see the price list.
