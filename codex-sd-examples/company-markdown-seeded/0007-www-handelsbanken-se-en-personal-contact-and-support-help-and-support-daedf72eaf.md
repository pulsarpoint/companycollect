##  FAQ
  * How do I log in using BankID on card?
You need a log-on card, a card reader and a cable to connect the card reader with your device. Follow the instructions on the following page:
[Install BankID Security Application and software driver](https://www.handelsbanken.se/en/personal/contact-and-support/help-and-support/installation-card-reader-with-cable)
  * How do I activate the mobile app?
If you wish to log in to the app using a personal code, you first need to activate your app. 
[Activate mobile app](https://www.handelsbanken.se/en/personal/contact-and-support/help-and-support/activate-the-mobile-app)
  * How do I order Mobile BankID?
To order a Mobile BankID for the first time, you need to log in to Online Banking using the card reader with or without the cable attached.
If you already have a Mobile BankID , you can order or renew your Mobile BankID in our mobile app.
[Order or renew Mobile BankID](https://www.handelsbanken.se/en/personal/everyday-banking/digital-services/mobile-bankid)
