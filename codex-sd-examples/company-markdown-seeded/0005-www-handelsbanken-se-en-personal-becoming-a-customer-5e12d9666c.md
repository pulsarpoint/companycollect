## Good to know before you become a customer
## Identification
Identify yourself with valid ID. We accept a Swedish passport, driving license, national ID card, or a valid foreign passport. Other official photo ID showing citizenship may also be accepted. Your branch may request additional documents.
![Two women talking. ](/tron/public/info/contents/v1/image/35-148656?variantId=900)
## We are required to know you as a customer
According to Swedish anti-money laundering law, we must know our customers and we ask questions when you become a customer. All customers are asked, and your answers are confidential and protected by banking secrecy.
[Why we ask questions](/en/personal/becoming-a-customer/why-we-ask-questions)
