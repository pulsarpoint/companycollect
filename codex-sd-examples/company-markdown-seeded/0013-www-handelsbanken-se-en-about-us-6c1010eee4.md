#  About Handelsbanken
Our bank is built on a strong local presence and close relationships.
  * Established in 1871
  * Nationwide branch network across four home markets
  * Over 12,000 employees
  * Highly decentralised organisation with the customer’s needs at its core
  * Higher customer satisfaction than comparable banks

## Nationwide branch network
All business decisions are made close to you, by those who know you and the local marketplace. We are available where you want us, on your mobile, online or at one of our branch offices. You can also get personal service around the clock over the phone.
