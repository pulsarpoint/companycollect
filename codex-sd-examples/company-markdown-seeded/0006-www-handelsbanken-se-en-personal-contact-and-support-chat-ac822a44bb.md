#  Chat with us
The chat is open to personal customers every day from 08:00-22:00. You can ask general questions about cards, accounts, payments and our digital services.
  * You always chat with a person.
  * Do not share personal data, account numbers or passwords.
  * We cannot answer questions about you, your accounts or your banking matters.
  * The chat is not connected to your login, even if you start it from a logged-in page.
