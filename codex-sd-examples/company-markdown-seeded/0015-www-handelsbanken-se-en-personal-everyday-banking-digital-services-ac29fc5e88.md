## Log-in methods
## Using Mobile BankID
Mobile BankID is a digital identification on your phone or tablet. Use it to log in to our digital services and as identification when calling Customer Service. Since it is a digital ID, you can also use it to log in to other companies and authorities.  
  
The first time you order a Mobile BankID you need to log in to Online Banking. Please ensure you have a Swedish passport or a Swedish national ID card at hand.   
  
If you already got a Mobile BankID, but a new device, you can order a new Mobile BankID in the app. 
[Log in to order your first Mobile BankID](https://secure.handelsbanken.se/logon/se/priv/en/bankid/)
![Business man looking at his phone.](/tron/public/info/contents/v1/image/35-277733?variantId=900)
## BankID on a card
Sometimes you need to identify yourself with a log-on card with BankID, for example when you order a Mobile BankID.   
  
Your log-on card incorporates a BankID, but only with the cable connected. However, to be able to use your BankID, you must download the BankID security application and drivers for the card reader to your computer.  
  
If you are already a customer and have downloaded the security program and driver, you can log in immediately. If you don’t already have a log-on card, you must visit your branch office
[Install the BankID security program](https://www.handelsbanken.se/en/personal/contact-and-support/help-and-support/install-bankid)
![Woman talking on the phone.](/tron/public/info/contents/v1/image/35-276324?variantId=900)
## Personal code 
You can use a personal code to identify yourself when using our phone services and to log in to the mobile app.   
  
With a personal code you can do your banking transactions at any time in the app or by phone. You can call us 24/7 to get support and help. You will also get access to our automated phone service.  
  
Log in to Online Banking to choose your personal code. You can change your code whenever you like.
[Log in to choose your personal code](https://secure.handelsbanken.se/logon/se/priv/sv/)
![Girl at a desk looking at her phone.](/tron/public/info/contents/v1/image/35-274826?variantId=900)
