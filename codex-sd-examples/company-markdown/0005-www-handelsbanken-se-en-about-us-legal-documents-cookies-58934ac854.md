#  Cookies
Here in our cookie policy you will find information about what cookies are, which cookies Handelsbanken uses on our website and for what purposes we use cookies. You will also find information about which cookies you have consented to and how to revoke or change your consent to cookies.
## Cookie policy
In this policy, the term “cookies” is used as a collective name for all types of storage and tracking technologies that mean that we gain access to and store certain information from the browser on the device you are using.
This cookie policy was last updated on 19 December 2025.
### What are cookies? 
Cookies are data that is saved on, or retrieved from, your computer, mobile phone or similar, usually by your browser when you visit a website that uses cookies. Cookies can be used to track a user's activity on a website in order to, for example, enable secure logins, collect statistics on how a website is used or adapt the website to the user's needs.
Cookies are saved for different lengths of time depending on the type of cookie you have chosen to consent to. In the List of cookies below you can see the storage times for all cookies that Handelsbanken uses on its website.
Some cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be opted out. Visitors to Handelsbanken's website can otherwise choose whether they want to accept the use of cookies, for one or more different purposes such as analysis, website customization or marketing.
You always have the option to change or revoke your consent at a later time through the settings above. If you withdraw your consent for all purposes, all non-essential cookies will be deleted.
In some cases, the use of cookies means that we process your personal data. In these cases, we follow our Privacy Notice.

Essential cookies are needed for the website to function securely and cannot be opted out. Essential cookies enable important functions such as login, language selection and navigation and are always turned on.
Without these cookies, the functionality of Handelsbanken's website would be limited and it would not be possible to use Handelsbanken's logged-in services. We also use Essential cookies to know whether you have previously consented to any or all purposes (see below). You cannot consent to essential cookies, but these are always turned on.
