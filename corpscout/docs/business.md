Asset 1 — The meta-demo cold email

The whole trick:

you've already run the product on their turf before you email.

The email isn't a pitch, it's a delivery. The sample must be

real

rows — that's the proof.

Subject lines (A/B these — short, lowercase, specific)

20 {{city}} businesses that need a website


found you 20 leads in {{city}}

{{first_name}}, 20 companies near you with no site

Email (web-studio version)

Hi {{first_name}},

I run Corpscout — we cross-map the {{country}} business register against companies' actual web presence.

I ran it for

{{city}}

and pulled

{{N}} active businesses with real revenue and no website

(or a broken one). That's exactly who {{agency_name}} builds for, so here are 3, unfiltered:

{{Company A}}

— construction, ~€800k revenue, no site — {{role_email_A}}

{{Company B}}

— {{industry_B}}, ~€{{rev_B}}, DIY builder, no HTTPS — {{role_email_B}}

{{Company C}}

— {{industry_C}}, ~€{{rev_C}}, no site — {{role_email_C}}

{{N}}

{{N}} {{city}} businesses with a live site but zero marketing setup

Follow-ups (same thread, no new sample needed)

-

+3 days:

"Bumping this — want me to send the full {{N}} for {{city}}, or filter to a specific niche (e.g. trades, restaurants, clinics)?" -

+7 days:

"Last one — I can leave you a free 10-row sample for any niche you name. Reply with the niche and it's yours."

Sending notes (deliverability = survival)

- Send from a

separate warmed domain

(e.g.

try-corpscout.com

), not your main — never risk the brand's inbox reputation. -

Low volume, high relevance

— 20–40/day/inbox, plain text, one CTA, one link. -

Role-based contacts only

(

info@

,

kontakt@

) in the sample — never a named person's address. This is your GDPR posture

and

it's in the email as trust. - The sample rows must be

accurate

— one wrong "you have no website" (they do) kills the demo. Pull from

Tier-A/B links only

for outreach.
