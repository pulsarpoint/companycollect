What is GapScore?

GapScore is a 0–100 measure of a company's digital presence

— how complete, modern, and marketed its public web footprint is. It's built for one job: separating businesses that have their digital house in order from those with a fixable gap.

Low score = bigger opportunity.

A company at 15 has revenue and no real website; one at 90 is fully built, secured, and marketed.

How it's calculated

Presence

Content & SEO

│ 15 │ Meta descriptions, structured data, mobile-readiness, search authority │ ├───────────────────┼────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

│ 5 │ A working contact on the company's own domain (vs. a free mailbox / none) │ └───────────────────┴────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Maturity tiers (the headline)

Every company also gets an archetype — more actionable than the number alone: ┌───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ Tier │ Meaning │ ├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

T0 — No presence

│ Active company, real revenue, no website found │ ├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

T1 — Placeholder

│ Parked page or social profile only │ ├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

T2 — DIY / outdated

│ Site-builder or ageing CMS, often no HTTPS │ ├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

T3 — Basic but unmarketed

│ Real modern site, but no analytics/ads/SEO │ ├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │

T4 — Mature

│ Modern, secure, measured, marketed │ └───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Where the data comes from

-

Official national business registers

(open data) — identity, status, industry, and financials. -

Public web crawl

— the company's live pages, headers, and technologies, as any visitor sees them. -

Cross-linking

— companies are matched to their websites by shared, verifiable identifiers (VAT, LEI, registration number) first, then corroborating signals. Every link carries a confidence tier.

What we deliberately don't do

-

No personal data.

Contacts are role-based business addresses (

info@

), never individuals' personal emails. -

No active scanning.

We observe the public web as archived — we never probe, log in, or touch a company's systems. -

No guessing on "no website."

A company is only marked T0 when the register lists none

and

no site could be confidently linked. We'd rather say "unknown" than be wrong.
