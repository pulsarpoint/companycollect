# Chile license notes

`datos.gob.cl` resources are public open-data resources, but the exact per-resource
license/reuse statement should be captured before redistribution. Treat the RES
CSV as usable for internal ingestion pending license confirmation.

SII files are publicly downloadable tax/statistical files. Confirm SII reuse terms
before redistributing raw files or derived public products.

Personal or ownership/composition data from SII should be reviewed before broad
processing.
