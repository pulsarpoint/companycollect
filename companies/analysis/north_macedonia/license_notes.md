# License & terms — North Macedonia

## Summary

The Central Registry (**CRM**) is the official source but **commercially
distributes** registry and financial data. Only a **free basic per-company search**
is open; bulk extracts, detailed data, and financial statements are **paid**. Treat
reuse/redistribution terms as **restricted** (governed by CRM's distribution terms).

## Per source

### CRM — Trade Registry & Registry of Annual Accounts (`crm.com.mk`)
- Official register + financial statements. **Free public search** (Пребарување)
  returns basic identity/status. **Bulk and detailed data, and annual accounts, are
  paid** via the CRM data-distribution service (subscription / per-document). No
  open bulk; redistribution governed by CRM's commercial terms — do not assume reuse
  rights. Catalogued from public documentation only (no raw values copied; the host
  was unreachable from this environment).

### UJP — Public Revenue Office (`ujp.gov.mk`)
- Administers ЕДБ / ДДВ (VAT). Per-company tax/VAT verification; no open bulk; reuse
  terms not stated.

### data.gov.mk
- Openly published datasets (CKAN) where present, reusable with attribution; does
  not host the full register. Unreachable here to confirm specifics.

## Personal data

The Trade Registry exposes **founders/managers (основачи/управители)** — personal
data when natural persons (North Macedonia Law on Personal Data Protection). These
must be **redacted** in committed outputs. Because the register was unreachable, no
per-company values were captured; the sample carries only **public-knowledge legal
names** with **null identifiers** (nothing fabricated).

## Practical guidance

- Use the CRM **free search** for identity/status; use **paid distribution** for
  bulk + financials under CRM's terms.
- Do not scrape; do not assume redistribution rights for CRM data.
- Currency **MKD**; dates dd.mm.yyyy; Cyrillic + Albanian.
