# Turkey — License Notes

## MERSIS (Ministry of Trade) — free per-company, no open bulk

- The central registry's per-company query (by MERSIS no / VKN / title) is publicly
  accessible at no charge. There is **no open bulk download or API**; bulk access
  is not offered to the public.
- Treatment here: **free per-company / no open bulk**. Company identity (MERSIS no,
  title, VKN, NACE, address) is corporate data; directors/shareholders (gazette /
  trade-registry records) are personal data (KVKK) — redact.

## Trade Registry Gazette (TOBB) — public

- The official gazette is public; registrations/changes are searchable per company.
  Reuse of gazette content should follow the gazette's terms; no open bulk.
- Treatment here: **useful_secondary_source** (per-company events).

## KAP (Public Disclosure Platform) — public disclosure

- KAP publishes statutory disclosures and financial statements of **listed**
  companies; public. Reuse is governed by KAP/Borsa İstanbul terms; verify before
  redistribution.
- Treatment here: **public** (listed only). Member list + financials browsable.

## GİB (Revenue Administration) — per-company

- VKN / KDV (VAT) taxpayer lookup per company; no open bulk.
- Treatment here: **useful_secondary_source**.

## Personal data

- **Directors / shareholders** (gazette, trade-registry records) are **personal
  data** under Turkey's **KVKK** (Personal Data Protection Law). Redact in shared
  samples. The committed sample contains only listed-company names (KAP).

## Tax identifiers

- The **VKN** (10-digit) is the tax id. Turkey has **VAT (KDV)** but **no separate
  VAT number** — the VKN serves as the tax/VAT identifier.
