# Qatar License Notes

## QFC Public Register (QFCA)

- Official public register published by the Qatar Financial Centre Authority.
- Browser-public, no login or payment. Intended for public verification of QFC-licensed
  firms and approved individuals.
- **No explicit open/reuse license** was located. Public visibility ≠ permission to
  redistribute. Treat as **unknown_public_register**; confirm reuse terms before bulk reuse.
- Approved-individual **names and addresses** are **personal data** — redact in any stored
  profile.

## MoCI Commercial Registration

- The onshore registry (CR number) is operated by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
- No open bulk/API; verification is a per-CR lookup. Treat access as **restricted**.
- Owners, partners, and managers are **personal data** — redact. Field model documented
  from public knowledge only; no per-company values captured.

## Qatar Stock Exchange (QSE)

- Listed-company disclosures and financial statements are **public regulatory disclosures**.
- Browser-public; no clean open API. Attribution to QSE / the issuer. Reuse of bulk
  disclosures should follow QSE terms.

## Qatar Open Data Portal (data.gov.qa)

- Opendatasoft portal operated by the Planning and Statistics Authority; generally open
  terms for the statistical datasets. **Not company data**, so not used as a company source.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: no authentication, postback, AJAX, CAPTCHA, or WAF was defeated.
- Redact all personal data (approved individuals, owners, partners, managers) per Qatar's
  Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016).
