# Vietnam — License Notes

## NBRP (National Business Registration Portal)

- The portal provides **free per-company public viewing**, but publishes **no open
  re-use licence** and **no open bulk/API**. Treat the data as **restricted for
  re-use** absent an agreement. The search is **CAPTCHA-gated** on submit — do not
  bypass it or run automated queries against it.
- **Bulk** access to the national business-registration database is via a **paid
  MOU** with the Business Registration Support Centre — contract terms govern
  re-use. Planning-only.
- **Personal data**: the **legal representative** name (người đại diện) is
  personal data — redact/minimise in any stored/published profile.

## GDT taxpayer lookup

- Per-company tax-code validation, CAPTCHA-gated, no documented open re-use/bulk.
  Use only for confirming a known company's status; do not bypass the CAPTCHA.

## HOSE / HNX / SSC (listed-company financials)

- Issuer disclosures are **public to view** but governed by the exchanges'/SSC's
  disclosure terms — not an open-data licence. Per-issuer; **listed companies
  only**. Confirm redistribution terms before republishing.

## GSO Enterprise Survey

- **Access-controlled statistical microdata** (research). Not a per-company
  register; not for redistribution as company records.

## Aggregators (masothue.com, infodoanhnghiep.com, vietstock, cafef, fireant)

- **Restricted / vendor terms**; they scrape/repackage official data. License
  uncertain — cross-check only; no raw values copied here.

## Summary

- **No open data**: the register, tax lookup, and financials are all view-only/
  gated/paid; there is **no lawful open bulk** of Vietnamese companies.
- **Personal-data caution**: legal-representative names.
- **Financials**: listed-only (HOSE/HNX/SSC); non-listed not published.
