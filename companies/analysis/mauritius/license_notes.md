# Mauritius License Notes

## data.govmu.org — List of ICT Companies (Open Data)

- Licensed **CC-BY-SA-4.0** (confirmed on the CKAN dataset). Reuse permitted, including
  commercial use, with **attribution** and **share-alike**. Attribute the publisher (MDPA) /
  data.govmu.org. This is the one clearly reusable company dataset.
- No personal data (company name + address + sector). The two ICT datasets (plain + GPS)
  share these terms.

## CBRD — CBRIS Online Search

- The CBRD register is authoritative but its online search is **Cloudflare Turnstile-gated**
  and document/file purchase is **paid** under the CBRD fee schedule. Treat access as
  **restricted/paid**; confirm any data-sharing terms with CBRD/MNS before reuse.
- Directors and shareholders are natural persons under Mauritius's **Data Protection Act
  2017** — redact in any stored profile.

## Stock Exchange of Mauritius (SEM)

- Listed-company information (published accounts, announcements) is **public market
  disclosure**, browser-public. Follow SEM terms for reuse and attribute SEM / the issuer.

## data.govmu.org portal

- National CKAN portal; dataset licenses vary (the ICT datasets are CC-BY-SA-4.0). Check the
  per-dataset license before reuse.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: the CBRD Turnstile/CAPTCHA and paid document services were **not**
  circumvented; only the openly-licensed CKAN CSV was downloaded.
- Redact natural-person data (directors, shareholders) per the Data Protection Act 2017.
- The **BRN** is a public company identifier, not personal data.
