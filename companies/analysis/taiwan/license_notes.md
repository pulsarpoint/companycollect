# Taiwan License Notes

## GCIS (MOEA Department of Commerce)

- The GCIS OpenData service publishes company-registration public data under Taiwan's
  **Open Government Data License** (政府資料開放授權條款), which permits reuse including
  commercial use with **attribution**. Confirm the current license version and attribution
  wording on the dataset page before redistribution.
- Company registration data is **public disclosure** under the Company Act. The
  **Responsible_Name** (legal representative) is a natural person — handle per Taiwan's
  **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA / 個人資料保護法)**; redact in stored profiles and
  committed samples.

## TWSE (Taiwan Stock Exchange) OpenAPI

- TWSE OpenAPI datasets are published as **open data** (Open Government Data License, Taiwan)
  for public reuse with attribution. Listed-company basic info is regulatory disclosure.
- Chairman / GM / spokesperson / signing auditor are natural persons — redact (PDPA).

## TPEx (Taipei Exchange) OpenAPI

- TPEx OpenAPI datasets are published as **open data** (Open Government Data License, Taiwan).
  OTC listed-company basic info is regulatory disclosure. Chairman / GeneralManager are
  natural persons — redact (PDPA).

## data.gov.tw

- National open-data platform under the Open Government Data License, Taiwan. Indexes the
  GCIS and exchange datasets.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: all access was via public open APIs; no authentication, payment,
  CAPTCHA, or rate-limit evasion.
- Redact all natural-person names (responsible person, directors/supervisors, chairman,
  GM, spokesperson, auditors) per the PDPA in stored/committed outputs.
- Attribution: cite "MOEA GCIS 商工登記公示資料", "Taiwan Stock Exchange OpenAPI", and
  "Taipei Exchange OpenAPI" as applicable.
