# License & terms — Morocco

## Summary

Casablanca Bourse listed disclosures are **public**; OMPIC company data is **paid**
(detailed data + Bilans + API) with a **reCAPTCHA-gated** free search; data.gov.ma is
openly licensed but has no company register. Treat OMPIC reuse/redistribution as
**restricted**.

## Per source

### Casablanca Stock Exchange (`casablanca-bourse.com`)
- Issuer listing and financial publications are **public** (mandatory disclosure).
  Attribution to the Bourse / issuer. Be polite with request volume. Listed companies
  only.

### OMPIC / directinfo (`directinfo.ma`)
- Official Registre Central du Commerce. The **free basic search is reCAPTCHA-gated**;
  **detailed company data, Bilans (financial statements), statistics, and the OMPIC
  API (CAPI)** are **paid/subscription**. No open bulk; no stated bulk-reuse rights.
  **Do not bypass the reCAPTCHA or the paywall.** Field model from public knowledge —
  **no real values copied**.

### data.gov.ma
- National open-data portal under an open-government license (attribution to the
  publisher), but no company-register dataset (statistics only).

## Personal data

OMPIC company records include **dirigeants (managers/directors)** and **associés
(shareholders/partners)** — personal data under **Morocco's Law 09-08 on the
protection of individuals with regard to personal data**. These must be **redacted**
in committed outputs. Because OMPIC detail is paid/gated, **no per-company OMPIC
values were captured**; the sample uses **Casablanca-verified + public-knowledge
listed companies** with **null OMPIC identifiers** (nothing fabricated).

## Practical guidance

- Use **Casablanca Bourse** (open) for listed companies + financials; use paid
  **OMPIC/directinfo** (subscription API or per-company) for the rest.
- Do not bypass the OMPIC reCAPTCHA/paywall; do not assume registry reuse rights.
- Currency **MAD**; French + Arabic; dates dd/mm/yyyy.
