# Australia — License Notes

## ABR / ABN Lookup (Bulk Extract + web services)

- **License: CC-BY 3.0 Australia** (Creative Commons Attribution). Free reuse incl.
  commercial, with **attribution** — credit "Australian Business Register" /
  "Australian Taxation Office" and the source URL + retrieval date. The bulk
  extract is published **weekly** on data.gov.au.
- The **web services** require a **free GUID** (registration token) but are not a
  payment; respect fair-use. The same public ABN fields apply.
- **Personal data**: ABR records for **individuals / sole traders** carry person
  **names** (GivenName/FamilyName). Treat as personal data (Privacy Act 1988) —
  redact/minimise individual names; company-level fields (ABN, ACN, org name,
  state, GST) are not personal data.

## ASIC (company register + financial reports)

- **Paid**: company-register extracts and financial-report documents are bought
  per item via **ASIC Connect** — not open data; do not redistribute purchased
  documents beyond the licence terms. Some ASIC datasets (e.g. **Business Names
  register**, banned/disqualified persons) are published **free** on data.gov.au
  under CC-BY.
- **Officeholders** in ASIC extracts are personal data — redact.

## ASX (listed-company financials)

- Listed issuers' financial reports/announcements are **public to view** but
  governed by ASX/issuer terms — not a blanket open-data licence. Listed only.

## Financial-data availability (important)

- Australia has **no universal public financial-filing obligation** for companies:
  **small proprietary companies generally do not lodge** financial reports
  publicly. Public companies, **large proprietary** companies, disclosing entities
  and registered schemes lodge with ASIC (**paid** to access). Listed companies
  disclose via ASX. So open structured financials are essentially **listed-only**.

## Summary

- **Open & usable (attribute, CC-BY 3.0 AU)**: ABR Bulk Extract + ABN Lookup API.
- **Paid**: ASIC company register + financial reports.
- **Public but issuer-termed**: ASX listed financials.
- **Personal-data caution**: sole-trader/individual names; ASIC officeholders.
- **No separate VAT id** — GST registration is the indirect-tax flag.
