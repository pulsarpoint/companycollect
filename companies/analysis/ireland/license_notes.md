# Ireland — License & Terms Notes

> Ireland's CRO company data — Company Records and the Financial-Statements index — is open under **CC-BY 4.0**.
> Reuse (incl. commercial) is allowed **with attribution**. The actual financial-statement PDFs are paid
> (pay-per-call); beneficial ownership is restricted.

## CRO Open Data Portal (opendata.cro.ie) — CC-BY 4.0
- Launched late 2024 under the **Open Data Directive**; the **Company Records** and **Financial Statements**
  datasets are published in open machine-readable formats under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0)**.
  **Attribute the CRO** when redistributing.
- Bulk download + CKAN API, updated daily. Also mirrored on **data.gov.ie** and **data.europa.eu**.
- **Basic company and submission data are free**; **document retrieval** (the actual filed PDFs) is available to
  **registered account holders on a pay-per-call basis**.

## Financial Statements
- The open **Financial Statements** dataset is the **filings index** (which company filed, which submission,
  which PDF, for which period) — **CC-BY 4.0**, free. The **figures** are inside the filed **PDFs**, retrieved
  **pay-per-call**. Small/micro companies file **abridged** accounts. Currency EUR.

## RBO (Register of Beneficial Ownership)
- Access to the beneficial-ownership register was **restricted** after the 2022 CJEU ruling (general public
  access withdrawn; designated persons / legitimate interest). **Not** open. Personal data (GDPR).

## Revenue / VIES
- Irish VAT (`IE` + 7 digits + 1–2 letters) is validated via VIES/Revenue. Validation only; not redistributable
  as a list. Not present in the CRO open data.

## data.gov.ie
- National open-data portal; harvests the CRO datasets (CC-BY 4.0). The authoritative source is opendata.cro.ie.

## Personal data / GDPR
- The open Company Records do **not** include directors/officers (those are in filed documents). Any
  person data fetched from documents, or from the RBO, is **personal data** — apply a GDPR lawful basis +
  retention; no direct-marketing reuse.

## Summary recommendation
- **Free to reuse (incl. commercial) with attribution**: Company Records + Financial-Statements index (CC-BY 4.0).
- **Paid**: financial-statement PDFs (document retrieval pay-per-call); structured figures at scale via a vendor.
- **Restricted**: RBO beneficial ownership.
- **Validation**: VIES/Revenue for VAT.
- Always **attribute the CRO**.
