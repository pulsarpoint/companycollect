# Georgia License Notes

## NAPR e-registry (National Agency of Public Registry)

- The company registry is public, but the free online search/extract is **CAPTCHA-gated**
  and the official API (`api.napr.gov.ge`) is **access-restricted** ("Access Denied").
  NAPR also sells data/extract services. Treat access as **restricted**; confirm reuse terms
  with NAPR before any redistribution.
- Directors and partners are natural persons under Georgia's **Law on Personal Data
  Protection** — redact in any stored profile.

## SARAS Reporting Portal (reportal.ge)

- Financial statements and management reports filed under the **Law of Georgia on Accounting,
  Reporting and Auditing** are **publicly available** for viewing. No explicit open/reuse
  license was located; the simple search requires an anti-forgery token. Treat as
  **public_disclosure (license unconfirmed)**; attribute SARAS / the filing entity and
  confirm bulk-reuse terms.

## Georgian Stock Exchange (GSE)

- Listed-securities information is **public market disclosure**. Browser-public. Follow GSE
  terms for reuse and attribute GSE / the issuer.

## data.gov.ge

- National open-data portal (Data Exchange Agency). License **unknown** — the portal was
  not reachable from this environment to confirm dataset terms.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: the NAPR CAPTCHA, the reportal anti-forgery token, and any access
  controls were **not** circumvented; the NAPR API "Access Denied" was respected.
- Redact natural-person data (directors, partners) per Georgia's data-protection law.
- The **9-digit identification code** is public (it is the company/tax id), not personal data.
