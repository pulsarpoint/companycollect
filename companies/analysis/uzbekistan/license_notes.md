# Uzbekistan License Notes

## EGRPO register (data.egov.uz / stat.uz)

- The Unified State Register of Enterprises and Organizations is published via the national
  **open-data portal data.egov.uz** (Statistics Agency). The portal's open-data terms would
  govern reuse, but **the portal was firewalled from this environment**, so the specific
  license/terms could **not be confirmed**. Treat as **unknown** until reachable; attribute
  the Statistics Agency / data.egov.uz.
- The register includes the **director's name** — a natural person under Uzbekistan's
  personal-data law — redact in any stored profile.
- The **STIR/INN** and EGRPO code are public company identifiers.

## State Tax Committee (soliq.uz)

- The taxpayer search and VAT-payers registry are public for verification, but **soliq.uz was
  firewalled** here. Treat reuse as **restricted** pending confirmation. Individual taxpayers
  are personal data — redact individuals.

## Republican Stock Exchange 'Toshkent' (UZSE)

- Listed-company information is **public market disclosure**, browser-public. Follow UZSE
  terms for reuse and attribute UZSE / the issuer.

## Statistics Agency (stat.uz)

- Statistical data under open terms; the per-company register is served via the firewalled
  data.egov.uz.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: the firewalled hosts (data.egov.uz, data.gov.uz, soliq.uz) were
  simply unreachable; UZSE was not scraped.
- Redact natural-person data (director names; individual taxpayers) per the personal-data law.
- The firewall is **environmental** (this network), not a real-world access restriction —
  these sources are expected to be open/usable from an unblocked network.
