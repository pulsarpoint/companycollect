# Iceland — License Notes

## Fyrirtækjaskrá (Skatturinn company register) — free per-company, paid bulk

- The **free per-company overview** (gjaldfrjálst yfirlit) is publicly accessible at
  no charge via the Skatturinn web search. It exposes the basic register data
  (name, kennitala, address, legal form, ÍSAT activity, VSK status, chair).
- **Bulk register extracts** and **certified certificates** (Staðfest vottorð) are
  **paid** — a fee schedule (gjaldskrá) applies. Redistribution of register data is
  governed by Skatturinn terms.
- Treatment here: **free per-company / paid bulk**. The basic identity fields are
  public; bulk is restricted. Company identity (kennitala, name, address, form) is
  corporate data; the **forráðamaður** (chair/responsible person) is **personal
  data (GDPR)** and is redacted in committed samples.

## Ársreikningaskrá (Annual Accounts Register) — public disclosure, paid retrieval

- Companies file annual accounts electronically for **public disclosure** ("til
  opinberrar birtingar"). The statements are public, but retrieval is **paid
  per-document**; no open bulk/XBRL.
- Treatment here: **blocked_by_payment** (financials). Cataloged from public docs.

## VAT (VSK) — per-company

- VAT-register (VSK) status is shown within the per-company register view. The VSK
  number is a separate registration. No open bulk.

## island.is / opingogn.is — open data portal

- The national open-data portal (opingogn.is → island.is) uses open terms for its
  hosted datasets, but does **not** openly host the company register. Cataloged for
  completeness.

## Personal data

- The **forráðamaður** (responsible person / board chair) and any officers are
  **personal data** under Iceland's data-protection law (GDPR/EEA) — redact in
  committed/shared samples. The committed sample redacts the chair's name. The
  **kennitala** of a legal entity is a corporate identifier; an individual's
  kennitala is personal data.

## Tax identifiers

- The **kennitala** is the company id and the tax id. Iceland has **VAT (VSK)**; the
  VAT number is a **separate** registration (not the kennitala).
