# Austria — License & Terms Notes

> Public ≠ unrestricted. Austria's authoritative data is largely paid; the open layer is limited.

## Firmenbuch (BMJ / courts) — PAID/contractual
- The free **brief extract** (aktueller Teilauszug) via JustizOnline is for individual viewing. Full
  extracts, documents, and bulk access are provided **for a fee via Verrechnungsstellen** under
  contractual terms — **not open data**. Do not assume rights to bulk-extract or redistribute.
- The **JustizOnline Firmenbuch API** is free but requires **ID Austria** (Austrian identity) — usable only
  by AT-identified parties; reuse still subject to the portal terms.

## Jahresabschluss (annual accounts) — PAID
- Publicly accessible **per document for a fee** via the clearing houses. No bulk redistribution rights
  implied; documents are individually paid.

## GISA — "Gewerbe in Österreich" (data.gv.at) — OPEN
- Published on data.gv.at as **open data** (Cooperation OGD Österreich; typically **CC-BY 4.0** —
  **confirm the exact license** on the dataset page). **Active trade authorizations WITHOUT personal data.**
  Free reuse with attribution. Excludes natural-person sole-trader personal data by design.

## Ediktsdatei / Insolvenzdatei — free queries; feed licensed
- Free **web queries** of the insolvency gazette. The **structured JSON feed** (iwg.justiz.gv.at) requires
  an **IWG re-use licence** (Informationsweiterverwendungsgesetz) — a formal application; the data may carry
  conditions/fees. Treat the structured feed as licensed, not open.

## WiEReG (beneficial ownership) — restricted
- Access is **restricted/fee-based** (legitimate interest / authorities / obliged entities). **Not** open.

## data.gv.at
- License is **per dataset** (often CC-BY 4.0). Check each dataset's license field.

## Commercial aggregators (Compass, KSV1870, firmafind, D&B)
- Proprietary, paid, per-vendor contract. Redistribution prohibited without a license. They resell
  Firmenbuch + Jahresabschluss.

## Personal data / GDPR
- The GISA open dataset deliberately **excludes personal data**. Firmenbuch officers (vertretungsbefugte
  Organe) and beneficial owners are natural persons — handle under GDPR if obtained from paid sources.

## Summary recommendation
- **Free to use now (with attribution)**: GISA "Gewerbe in Österreich" (data.gv.at), free brief Firmenbuch
  extract, free insolvency-gazette web queries.
- **Paid/contractual**: Firmenbuch full extracts, Jahresabschluss, and commercial aggregators — confirm
  redistribution rights before reuse. WiEReG is restricted.
