# Italy — License & Terms Notes

> Public ≠ freely reusable. Confirm before redistribution or commercial use.

## Registro Imprese / InfoCamere (authoritative) — PAID/contractual
- The Business Register data and documents (visure, **bilanci XBRL**, administrators) are provided under
  **per-document fees** and **contractual terms** via Telemaco and the InfoCamere Web Service/API.
- **Not open data.** Redistribution of purchased documents/records is restricted by the InfoCamere terms.
- Do not assume any right to bulk-extract or redistribute Registro Imprese content.

## Bilanci XBRL (financials) — PAID
- Annual accounts are official deposits retrieved per document for a fee. The XBRL/PDF prospetto is
  individually licensed/paid. No bulk redistribution rights implied.

## InfoCamere / CCIAA regional open data — CC-BY 4.0
- Datasets on the regional open-data portals (e.g. opendata.marche.camcom.it, "Open Data Imprese Italia")
  are **CC-BY 4.0**: free reuse incl. commercial, **with attribution** (cite the portal + "CCIAA … su
  dati InfoCamere"). These are **aggregate** statistics, not per-company data.

## Startup innovative & PMI innovative — IODL 2.0 / CC-BY
- The open lists are released under the **Italian Open Data Licence (IODL) 2.0** / CC-BY (attribution).
- The **bulk API** requires registration via **PEC** and acceptance of specific conditions of use —
  treat the API path as conditioned, the published XLS lists as open with attribution.

## ANAC procurement — open
- Open procurement data (CC-BY / IODL). Supplier names + CF/PIVA are reusable with attribution. Personal
  data of individual contractors may appear — handle under GDPR.

## ISTAT (ASIA) — CC-BY (aggregate)
- ISTAT content reusable with attribution; ASIA is aggregate (statistical confidentiality) — no
  per-company personal/commercial data concerns.

## GLEIF LEI — CC0
- GLEIF LEI data is **CC0 (public domain)** — unrestricted reuse. Covers only LEI-holding entities.

## Commercial aggregators (Cerved, AIDA/BvD, Atoka, CRIBIS) — PAID
- Proprietary, paid, per-vendor contract. Redistribution prohibited without a license. They resell
  Registro Imprese data plus their own enrichment/scores.

## Personal data / GDPR
- Administrators / directors (amministratori) and individual entrepreneurs are natural persons. Apply a
  GDPR lawful basis and retention policy before persisting names beyond the raw zone.

## Summary recommendation
- **Free to use now (with attribution)**: InfoCamere aggregate open data (CC-BY 4.0), startup/PMI
  innovative lists (IODL 2.0), ANAC suppliers, GLEIF LEI (CC0).
- **Paid/contractual**: Registro Imprese (incl. bilanci XBRL) and commercial aggregators — confirm
  redistribution rights before any reuse.
