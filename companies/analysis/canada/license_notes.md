# Canada — License Notes

## Corporations Canada — Federal Corporations (bulk + API)

- **License: Open Government Licence – Canada (OGL)**. Free reuse incl. commercial,
  with **attribution** — credit "Corporations Canada / Innovation, Science and
  Economic Development Canada" and retain source + retrieval date.
- Applies to both the **bulk CSVs** (open.canada.ca) and the **real-time API**.
- **Personal data**: the **directors** (names) returned by the API are personal
  data (Privacy Act / PIPEDA) — redact/minimise. The bulk CSV has only director
  **counts** (not personal data); names come from the API.
- Bilingual: corporate names have a "form 1" (often English) and "form 2" (often
  French) field.

## SEDAR+ (reporting-issuer financials)

- Filings are **public to view** (free) but governed by the CSA/SEDAR+ terms — not
  a blanket open-data licence. **Reporting issuers only** (public companies, funds).
  Confirm redistribution terms before republishing documents.

## Provincial registries

- **Licences vary by province**: Québec **REQ** publishes open data (Données
  Québec) and BC **OrgBook** is openly licensed; **Ontario, Alberta** and others
  may **charge** for searches/bulk and restrict reuse. Check each province's terms.

## Financial-data availability (important)

- **Private-company financials are not public** in Canada. Open financials exist
  only for **reporting issuers** (public companies / investment funds) via SEDAR+.

## Summary

- **Open & usable (attribute, OGL)**: federal bulk + API.
- **Public but issuer-termed**: SEDAR+ (reporting issuers).
- **Mixed/paid**: provincial registries (varies).
- **Personal-data caution**: director names (federal API; provincial registries).
- **No separate VAT id** — GST/HST registration uses the BN + RT program account.
