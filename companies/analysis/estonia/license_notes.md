# Estonia — License & Terms Notes

> Estonia's e-Business Register open data — including financial statements, beneficial owners and shareholders —
> is published under **CC-BY 4.0**. Reuse (incl. commercial) is allowed **with attribution**. Personal data is
> still governed by GDPR regardless of the licence.

## e-Business Register open data (RIK) — CC-BY 4.0
- Since **1 October 2022** all e-Business Register data is **free of charge** to everyone. Previously, data
  beyond the registry card (shareholders, articles, beneficial owners, annual reports) was paid.
- The open-data downloads and services are licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0)**.
  **Attribute** the source (Registrite ja Infosüsteemide Keskus / e-Business Register) when redistributing.
- The download portal requires **accepting the licence terms** before downloading; the data itself is then
  freely reusable under CC-BY 4.0.
- Covers entities with active, liquidation, or bankruptcy status (currently-registered entities).

## Financial statements (majandusaasta aruanne)
- Published as **structured open data** under the same CC-BY 4.0 — report metadata, balance-sheet/income-
  statement line items, and revenue breakdowns. No payment, no document paywall. Currency EUR.

## Beneficial owners (kasusaajad) & shareholders (osanikud)
- Both are available as **open bulk data** under CC-BY 4.0. This is unusual: after the 2022 CJEU ruling several
  EU states restricted public beneficial-ownership access, but Estonia continues to publish it openly.
- **GDPR still applies**: these are personal data. A lawful basis and a retention policy are required before
  persisting, and the data must not be reused for direct marketing. CC-BY governs IP reuse, not data protection.

## EMTA (Tax and Customs Board)
- KMKR (VAT) is already in the register basic data. EMTA additionally publishes tax-debt / paid-taxes open
  datasets. VIES validates a given KMKR. Validation/enrichment.

## avaandmed.eesti.ee
- National open-data catalog; licence is **per dataset**. The authoritative company source is
  avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee directly.

## Summary recommendation
- **Free to reuse (incl. commercial) with attribution**: all e-Business Register open datasets (company,
  financials, beneficial owners, shareholders, officers) under CC-BY 4.0.
- **GDPR care**: beneficial owners, shareholders and officers are personal data — lawful basis + retention; no
  direct marketing.
- Always **attribute RIK / e-Business Register**.
