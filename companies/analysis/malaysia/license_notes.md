# License & terms — Malaysia

## Summary

SSM **commercially distributes** company and financial data — profiles and
financials are **paid products** (e-Info / MyData-SSM); only a free e-Search
(existence) is open. data.gov.my is openly licensed but has no company register.
Treat SSM reuse/redistribution as **restricted** (governed by SSM's commercial
terms).

## Per source

### SSM e-Info / MyData-SSM (`ssm-einfo.my`, `mydata-ssm.com.my`)
- Official SSM channels. Company Profile / Business Profile / LLP Profile /
  **Financial Comparison** / **Financial Historical** / Company Financial Report are
  **paid per document** (MYR), behind SAML login. No open bulk/API; no stated
  bulk-reuse rights. Free e-Search is for existence verification only. Do not assume
  redistribution rights; do not bypass the paywall/login. Field lists here come from
  SSM's **published sample templates** — **no real company values copied**.

### data.gov.my (OpenDOSM)
- National open-data portal under an open-government license (attribution to DOSM).
  Statistics only; no company register.

### Bursa Malaysia (`bursamalaysia.com`)
- Listed-company disclosures are public (mandatory disclosure); attribution to
  Bursa/issuer. WAF-blocked for automation here; listed only.

## Personal data

SSM Company Profiles include **directors and shareholders** — personal data when
natural persons under Malaysia's **PDPA 2010** (Personal Data Protection Act). These
must be **redacted** in committed outputs. Because SSM data is paid, **no per-company
values were captured**; the sample uses **public-knowledge listed-company names**
with **null SSM identifiers** (nothing fabricated).

## Practical guidance

- Buy SSM profiles/financials per company (e-Info / MyData-SSM); use the free
  e-Search for existence checks; Bursa for listed financials.
- Do not bypass the SSM paywall/login or Bursa's WAF.
- Currency **MYR**; Malay + English; dates dd-mm-yyyy.
