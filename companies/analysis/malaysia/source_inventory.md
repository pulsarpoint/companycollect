## Identifiers

- **Company registration number** — **new 12-digit** (since 2019, e.g. 201901000005)
  or **old** NNNNNNN-A. SSM-issued.
- **ROB number** — sole proprietorship / partnership.
- **TIN** — income tax number (LHDN; companies prefixed `C`).
- **SST registration number** — Sales & Service Tax (no VAT/GST since 2018).

## Key facts

- **SSM** is the official register, but company profiles + **financials are paid
  products** (e-Info / MyData-SSM); only a free **e-Search** (existence) is open.
- **No open bulk register; no open financials.** data.gov.my has no company
  register (DOSM statistics); Bursa is listed-only + WAF-blocked here.
- Currency **MYR**; Malay + English. Directors/shareholders are personal data
  (PDPA 2010) → redact.
