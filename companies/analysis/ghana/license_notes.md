# License & terms — Ghana

## Summary

GSE listed disclosures are **public**; ORC company search/documents are **paid** (via
eServices); data.gov.gh was firewalled. Treat ORC reuse/redistribution as
**restricted**.

## Per source

### GSE (`gse.com.gh`)
- Listed-company directory, profiles, and financial statements are **public**
  (mandatory disclosure). Attribution to GSE / issuer. Be polite with request volume.
  Listed companies only.

### ORC / RGD eServices (`eservices.rgd.gov.gh`)
- Official registrar. Company search + documents (incorporation, status report,
  annual returns) are **paid per transaction** on the eServices portal. No open
  bulk/API; no stated bulk-reuse rights. Hosts firewalled from this environment. Do
  not bypass the login/paywall. Field model from public knowledge — **no real values
  copied**.

### data.gov.gh
- Firewalled at investigation time; nothing to license.

## Personal data

ORC company records include **directors and shareholders** — personal data under
Ghana's **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**. These must be **redacted** in
committed outputs. Because ORC data is paid/firewalled, **no per-company ORC values
were captured**; the sample uses **GSE-verified + public-knowledge listed companies**
with **null ORC identifiers** (nothing fabricated).

## Practical guidance

- Use **GSE** for listed companies + financials (open); buy **ORC** documents
  (search/status/annual returns) per company via eServices for the rest.
- Do not bypass the eServices login/paywall; do not assume ORC reuse rights.
- Currency **GHS**; English; dates dd/mm/yyyy.
