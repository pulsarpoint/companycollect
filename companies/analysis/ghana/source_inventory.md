# Source inventory — Ghana

| Source | Type | Org | Access | Formats | Financials | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE listed (`gse.com.gh`) | Listed companies + financials | Ghana Stock Exchange | **Open** | html, pdf | yes (listed) | **recommended** |
| ORC / RGD eServices (`eservices.rgd.gov.gh`) | Official registry + documents | ORC (ex-RGD) | eServices, paid; firewalled here | html, pdf | yes (annual returns, paid) | blocked_by_payment |
| data.gov.gh | Open-data portal | NITA | Firewalled here | — | no | unavailable |

## Identifiers

- **Company registration number** — ORC-issued.
- **TIN** — Tax Identification Number (GRA; businesses).
- **Ghana Card PIN** — individuals' tax/ID (replacing individual TINs).
- **Business registration** — sole proprietorships / partnerships.

## Key facts

- **ORC/RGD** is the official register but delivered via **eServices** — search +
  documents are **paid**; no open bulk/API; the hosts were **firewalled** from this
  environment.
- **GSE** is the one **open** source — listed companies + financial statements
  (verified live: Ecobank Ghana, GCB, AngloGold Ashanti, CalBank, Standard Chartered
  Ghana…). Listed only (~35).
- **data.gov.gh** firewalled. Currency **GHS**; English. Directors/shareholders are
  personal data (Act 843) → redact.
