# Albania — License Notes

## QKB commercial register — public register

- The QKB (National Business Center) commercial register is public; per-company
  extracts (ekstrakt) are free. The register is republished as **open data** by Open
  Data Albania (AIS).
- Treatment here: **public / open** for company identity. **Administrator / owners**
  (ortakë/aksionarë) are **personal data** (Albanian Law 9887 on personal data
  protection; aligned with GDPR) — redact in shared samples.

## Open Data Albania (opencorporates.al) — open data

- Open Data Albania / AIS publishes the QKB data as open data with company lists and
  per-company pages. Reuse is generally permitted; attribute Open Data Albania / QKB.
- Treatment here: **open / reusable with attribution**.

## opendata.gov.al — national open data

- The national portal uses open terms; the QKB dataset may be hosted there.

## Personal data

- **Administrator / owners** are **personal data** — redact. The committed sample
  redacts the administrator and contains only NIPT + company name.

## Tax identifiers

- The **NIPT/NUIS** is the company id, the tax id, **and the VAT id** (Albania has
  VAT; the NIPT serves as the VAT number).
