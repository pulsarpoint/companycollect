# Armenia License Notes

## State Register of Legal Entities (e-register.am)

- The company register is public (free online search), but the live service is **protected by
  Radware Bot Manager** and there is **no open bulk or API**. Treat access as **restricted**;
  confirm any reuse/data-sharing terms with the Ministry of Justice / State Register.
- Directors and founders are natural persons under Armenia's **Law on the Protection of
  Personal Data** — redact in any stored profile.

## State Revenue Committee (SRC) taxpayer search

- The SRC taxpayer search is browser-public for per-TIN verification (name/status, VAT status).
  No documented open bulk/API. Treat bulk reuse as **restricted** pending confirmation. The
  TIN and company name/status are generally public, but handle any natural-person taxpayer
  data per the personal-data law.

## Armenia Securities Exchange (AMX)

- Listed-securities information is **public market disclosure**, served via a JS SPA. Follow
  AMX terms for reuse and attribute AMX / the issuer.

## Open Data Armenia (data.opendata.am)

- Civic CKAN portal; dataset licenses vary and it is **not** an official register. Not used as
  a company source.

## General

- Nothing was bypassed: the State Register's Radware bot protection was **not** circumvented;
  the SRC search and AMX SPA were not scripted/scraped.
- Redact natural-person data (directors, founders) per Armenia's personal-data law.
- The **TIN (ՀՎՀՀ/HVHH)** and **state registration number** are public company identifiers.
